# Recovery Advantage imagery and motion

| Scene | Asset and source | Framing and light | Motion and fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enquiries | `night-{540,960}.webp`, generated for this project and used as an illustration | Night road behind a dimensional CSS phone | Expanding amber rings and a glass reflection frame the 24/7 enquiry-preparation tag. |
| Eight services | Original vector pictograms in `recovery-capabilities.mjs` and graphite surfaces in `recovery-kinetic.css` | An eight-position selector and a linked service preview share one surface. | Handling | `advantage-loading-540.webp` (540 × 264) and `advantage-loading-960.webp` (960 × 470), made with the built-in image generator for this project | Unbranded sedan and lowered carrier deck in one coherent depot composition, with labels anchored to the bumper clearance and deck entry | A directional reveal sets the scene. | Arrangements and backdrop | `dubai-szr-{840,1440}.webp`, [Sheikh Zayed Road at night by Michael Starkie](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-night-view-of-a-city-with-a-freeway-sTOQG-SAFqY) | A single editorial briefing over the color-graded Dubai photograph | A masked reveal introduces the briefing, followed by three detail rows and a light pass over the availability and quote step. |

The location photograph is used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The generated stills are project artwork and are not evidence of the business’s staff or fleet. Responsive WebP sources use low fetch priority, with CSS crops for the different layouts. The illustrations have empty alt text because each panel provides its meaning in adjacent text.

The section uses the project's installed [GSAP under its Standard License](https://gsap.com/standard-license/), with a CSS grid track transition for selection on wide screens. Handling and Arrangements reveal once when first in view, replay on selection, hover or keyboard focus, and settle while their ambient reflections continue. Offscreen playback pauses. Reduced motion displays the static compositions and retains the selection controls and enquiry link.

The Handling artwork was prompted as a full, unbranded graphite sedan at a shallow lowered flatbed in a charcoal recovery depot. Both visible tires are supported, with the front on the floor and the rear at the deck entry. The brief specified realistic scale, contact shadows, subdued workshop lighting and no text. [Miller Industries' low-angle carrier documentation](https://www.millerind.com/products/car-carriers/vla-carrier) provided a reference for the scene's stationary geometry.
